You will be learning about the Basics of Python, Math & Variables: 

>>> 2 + 2
4
>>> 2 +                 2

3936
>>> 201 / 3

This is how you can use math in Python.

Do you notice that 2 + 2 still works? Why?

Because it is still 2 + 2 except written longer.

You could try the same thing with a calculator on your PC.

Now next for variables: 

>>> num1 = 10
>>> num2 = 20
>>> num1 + num2
30
>>>

This works because num1 equals 10 and num2 equals 20.

num1 + num2 = 30

Same thing here: 10 + 20 = 30
